# PulseIQ — Task 7: Attrition Analytics

## Three biggest findings

1. Observed attrition: Legal & Compliance had the highest mean visible monthly attrition rate at 2.50% across 12 visible months.

2. Predicted risk, not actual exits: Customer Support had the highest governed high-risk share at 34.48% for a group of 58.

3. Predicted risk comparison: Marketing had a governed high-risk share of 16.67% for a group of 12.

## Exit-count privacy note

Only one department-month passed the governed M03 exit-count threshold: March 2025, with 5 released exits. This is not the total number of exits across the dataset. Suppressed counts were excluded.

## Prediction limitation

Predicted risk is an estimate for supportive HR review, not a certainty about any individual and not a basis for automated employment decisions.
