# CEO Questions — Workforce and Headcount Dashboard

1. How has workforce headcount changed over time?
2. Which departments have the largest share of the current workforce?
3. How many employees were hired during the selected period?
4. How is the workforce distributed across location tiers?
5. What is the current distribution of employees by work mode?

## How to Use the Dashboard

- Use the date filter to examine workforce trends over a selected period.
- Use the department filter to focus on a particular department.
- Compare the KPI cards with the charts to understand workforce size and composition.
- Interpret headcount, hires, and exits according to the metric definitions used by the governed views.

## Important Note

Headcount is a point-in-time measure. Hires and exits are period-based measures. They should not be treated as interchangeable metrics.
